Key takeaways

Silence is a signal you compute

  1. 01

    Seal first

    The ear tip blocks high frequencies on its own. Electronics only have to fight the lows.

    ~20 dB passive above 1 kHz
  2. 02

    Invert the wave

    The outer mic hears noise before your ear does; the chip plays it back flipped by 180°.

    feedforward
  3. 03

    Close the loop

    An inner mic measures what is left at your eardrum and corrects the estimate continuously.

    feedback
  4. 04

    Latency limits it

    Every microsecond of delay becomes phase error, which is why ANC shines below 1 kHz.

    ≈ 20 µs budget

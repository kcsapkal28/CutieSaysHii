Physiology · Circulation

Two loops, one pump

≈ 5 L/min pumped at rest: your whole blood volume, about once a minute

Stylised: the right side of the heart is drawn on the left, as if facing you. Not to scale.

Follow one red cell. It comes back tired from the body, gets pushed to the lungs, picks up oxygen, and only then goes back out.

The point: the heart is two pumps side by side. The right one feeds the lungs, the left one feeds everything else.

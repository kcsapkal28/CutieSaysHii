Typical UK figures, rounded: 12,000 kWh of heat a year; boiler 90% efficient; heat pump seasonal COP ≈ 3; gas and grid both ≈ 0.2 kg CO₂ per kWh. Illustrative.

Read the boiler column first: it is the baseline everyone knows. Then reveal the heat pump.

The trick is in row one: a heat pump moves heat from outside air instead of making it, so 1 kWh of electricity delivers about 3 kWh of heat.

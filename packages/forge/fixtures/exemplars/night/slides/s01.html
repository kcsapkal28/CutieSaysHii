Open A string, 110 Hz. Modes drawn full height; in the sum each falls as 1/n.

Pluck a guitar string and you do not get one frequency: you get 110 Hz plus 220, 330, 440, 550… all sounding together.

The top line is what the string really does: the sum of the five modes underneath. Their mix is why a guitar and a piano playing the same A sound different.

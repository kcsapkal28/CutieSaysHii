Five layers spread every wheel's weight

Typical UK build-up for a light-traffic road; thicknesses vary with traffic. Layer heights exaggerated, not to scale.

Start with the whole stack: what you drive on is only the top 40 mm.

Then peel it from the top. Each layer is coarser and thicker than the one above, so a wheel's load is spread wider and wider before it reaches the soil.

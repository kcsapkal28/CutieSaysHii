Remove the otter, lose the forest

Simplified web after Estes & Palmisano (1974) and later kelp-forest studies; links illustrative.

Step through slowly. The otter has only a handful of links, fewer than the crab, yet removing it unravels the whole forest.

That is what "keystone species" means: importance is not the number of links but where they sit.

Genetics · Hardy–Weinberg

Two allele shares fix all three genotype shares

p + q = 1, so (p + q)² = 1

p² + 2pq + q² = 1

Assumes random mating, no selection, migration or mutation. CF ≈ 1 in 2,500 births in Northern Europe; values rounded.

Build the equation one term at a time and point at the matching area of the square each time: the square IS the equation.

Last step: a rare disease (q² tiny) still means one person in 25 carries the allele. That is why carrier screening matters.

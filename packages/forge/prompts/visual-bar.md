## The visual bar

Every deck is judged against a set of hand-built reference explainers. Know them well; this is the level to hit.

1. **"Inside ANC" title.** Near-black violet backdrop with a soft radial glow top-right. Huge, thin, letter-spaced display title on the left ("INSIDE" in white, "ANC" in a cyan→violet gradient), a quiet one-line subtitle under it. On the right, one procedural 3D earbud: glossy white plastic, a translucent silicone ear tip, soft contact shadow on an invisible floor, slowly turning. A two-line caption is centred near the bottom. That's all: one object, one title, one sentence.
2. **Signal flow through an exploded earbud.** The same earbud exploded along one axis into ~10 parts: ear canal (translucent cone), ear tip, nozzle, speaker driver, inner chamber, PCB, ANC chip, amplifier, outer housing, battery, antenna. Four colour-coded particle streams (dotted beads moving along curved paths): noise (white) from outside, anti-noise (cyan) out of the processor, feedback correction (mint) and music (amber). Numbered callout chips ("09 ANC processor"), each with a hairline leader to a dot on its part. A tiny tracked-out kicker ("PASS 3 · HYBRID ANC"), a two-line claim, and a dot legend at the bottom.
3. **Exploded parts inventory.** Fourteen parts laid out in 3D space with real materials (green PCB with traces, gold antenna, blue battery wrap, metal mesh). Callout chips use a mono numeral in the accent colour plus the part name in the body font, on a dark surface pill with a left accent bar. Generous negative space; the caption sits at the bottom.
4. **"Sixty-Four Waves" (light theme).** A JPEG pipeline staged in 3D on a pale floor: an 8×8 input tile as a bar field, an arc labelled DCT, a 64-cell grid of cosine patterns, an arc labelled Σ, the rebuilt tile. Step tabs across the top (Shift −128, DCT, ÷Q round, ×Q, Σ rebuild), a quality slider with a live "36 of 64 rounded to 0" readout, and an explanation card with the formula.
5. **"Five one-way channels, each with its own handshake."** Big sentence-case claim as the title. Manager and Subordinate boxes either side; five coloured horizontal channels between them, each labelled with the signal names in small caps underneath; data packets (D0, D1, D2) slide along the W channel. Write/Read/Both toggle buttons; a short paragraph on the right.
6. **"Bridging SPI into AXI4-Stream: who can wait?"** A live simulation: a shift register filling bit by bit, a FIFO of configurable depth, packets (0xD7, 0xB2) moving downstream, TVALID/TREADY indicators, Delivered/Lost counters. Controls: slave/master mode, FIFO depth 2/4/8, "Make downstream busy". One sentence underneath states the consequence.
7. **"Any 8 × 8 tile is a mix of 64 patterns."** A pixel-art image with a tile grid; pick a tile; a slider adds DCT patterns one by one, coarse to fine; the reconstruction updates live next to the original; big stat readouts ("Energy captured 79.9%", "Avg error per pixel 34.3").

What they share:
- **One idea per slide**, stated as a sentence-case claim, and a visual that *is* the explanation. No bullet lists, no paragraphs of body text, no clip-art icons, no decorative stock imagery.
- **Bespoke mechanisms**: the visual is a working model of the thing (a FIFO that actually overflows, packets that actually move on their channel, a reconstruction that actually improves). The viewer learns by watching or poking it.
- **Procedural 3D done properly**: recognisable silhouettes from lathe/capsule/extrude geometry, physically based materials, a key light plus rim light, contact shadows, objects that fill 40–60% of the canvas. Never a tiny dark object lost in the void.
- **Labels that land**: every callout has a leader line ending exactly on its part; numbered chips; a legend whenever colour encodes meaning.
- **Restraint**: two typefaces, a near-monochrome base with one to three accents that each mean something, large type (titles 64–96px, captions 30–40px, labels 22–28px on the 1920×1080 canvas), lots of air.
- **Motion with purpose**: particles show flow direction, steps reveal causality one beat at a time, and nothing moves just to decorate.
